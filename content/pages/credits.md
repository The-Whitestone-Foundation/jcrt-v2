## Icons

Every icon on this site is an inline SVG. No icon font is loaded.

- [Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com/) by Helena Zhang and Tobias Fried, regular weight, supplies the menu, navigation, social and contact glyphs. [MIT license](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core/blob/main/LICENSE).
- [Academicons](https://jpswalsh.github.io/academicons/) by James Walsh supplies the ORCID iD glyph on author pages. Glyphs are under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://github.com/jpswalsh/academicons/blob/master/LICENSE).

## Design and typography

- The layout descends from [Editorial](https://html5up.net/editorial) by [HTML5 UP](https://html5up.net/), used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://html5up.net/license) license.
- Text is set in system fonts following [Modern Font Stacks](https://modernfontstacks.com/): an Old Style stack for body copy and a Slab Serif stack for headings. Nothing is downloaded.
