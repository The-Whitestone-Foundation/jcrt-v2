The *Journal for Cultural and Religious Theory* ([www.jcrt.org](http://www.jcrt.org/)) now has a blog.   It is called *JCRT Live*.

JCRT Live is a blog for editors, contributors, and other invited participants in conversations sponsored by the JCRT.  The JCRT is a peer-reviewed journal devoted to both disciplinary and interdisciplinary scholarship of a cutting-edge nature that deals broadly with the phenomenon of religious and cultural theory.

Regular blog conversations with the opportunity to respond by readers will be launched the week of September 22.

If you are interested in being a blog contributor, please email the editor at <editor@jcrt.org>
