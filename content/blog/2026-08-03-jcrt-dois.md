The Journal for Cultural and Religious Theory (JCRT) is pleased to announce that, beginning with our next issue, every article we publish will be assigned a Digital Object Identifier (DOI). We are also undertaking a retroactive project to assign DOIs to our back catalog, so that the full body of JCRT scholarship becomes persistently citable and discoverable.

![JCRT DOI Numbers graph](https://files.jcrt.org/images/jcrt-doi.png)

What's changing

-   All future publications will receive a DOI at the time of publication, registered through [Knowledge Commons Works](https://works.hcommons.org/) under the DOI prefix `10.17613`.

-   We will retroactively assign DOIs to previously published articles, working back through our archive issue by issue.

-   DOI assignment will roll out in phases; we will share updates as batches of legacy articles are completed.

Why this matters

-   Persistent, stable links: DOIs resolve to an article regardless of URL or platform changes, ensuring long-term access.

-   Improved discoverability: DOIs make JCRT scholarship easier to find, index, and cross-reference in databases, search engines, and citation managers (Zotero, EndNote, etc.).

-   Better citation tracking: DOIs allow citation counts and metrics to be tracked accurately across platforms like Google Scholar and Crossref.

-   Interoperability: DOIs integrate JCRT articles into the broader scholarly infrastructure, supporting indexing in services that require persistent identifiers.

What authors and readers can expect

-   Authors of upcoming articles will receive their DOI as part of the standard publication and notification process; no additional action is required.

-   Authors of previously published work will be notified once a DOI has been assigned to their article, and asked to update citations or CVs accordingly.

-   Readers can expect to see DOIs listed alongside article metadata on our site and in citation exports.

Timeline

-   New articles: DOIs active starting with volume 25, issue 2 (August 2026).

-   Legacy articles: rollout beginning August 2026, proceeding by volume and issue.

**Update, August 30, 2026:** the retroactive rollout is complete. All 829 articles in the numbered archive, from volume 1 in 1999 onward, now have DOIs. See [Every Article in the JCRT Archive Now Has a DOI](/blog/2026-08-30-jcrt-archive-doi-complete/).
